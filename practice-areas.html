Comprehensive Legal Services

PALM LEGAL offers dedicated legal counsel and representation across a broad range of practice areas before the Supreme Court of India, High Courts, Tribunals, and District Courts.

Practice Domain 01

Civil Litigation

Comprehensive representation in civil disputes before Trial Courts, High Courts, and the Supreme Court of India.

Key Focus & Remedies

  • Property and title disputes
  • Recovery suits and money decrees
  • Injunctions — temporary and permanent
  • Declaratory suits and specific performance
  • Civil appeals and revisions
  • Execution of decrees

Criminal Law

Defence and prosecution in criminal matters across all tiers of the Indian judiciary.

Key Focus & Remedies

  • Regular and anticipatory bail applications
  • Criminal appeals and revisions
  • Quashing of FIR and criminal proceedings
  • Cases under IPC / BNS, CrPC / BNSS, POCSO, NDPS
  • White-collar crimes and economic offences
  • Cheque dishonour matters (Section 138 NI Act)
Enquire On Criminal Matter Trial & High Courts
Practice Domain 03

Constitutional Law & Writs

Filing and arguing writ petitions before High Courts and the Supreme Court for enforcement of fundamental rights.

Key Focus & Remedies

  • Writ Petitions (Habeas Corpus, Mandamus, Certiorari, Quo Warranto)
  • Public Interest Litigation (PIL)
  • Special Leave Petitions (SLP) before Supreme Court
  • Fundamental rights enforcement (Articles 14, 19, 21)
  • Constitutional validity challenges
  • Matters of public importance

  • Maintenance and alimony
  • Child custody and visitation rights

  • Hindu Succession and inheritance disputes
  • Adoption and guardianship

Service & Labour Law

Representation for government employees, private sector workers, and employers in service and employment matters.

Key Focus & Remedies

  • Service disputes before CAT and High Courts
  • Departmental inquiry and disciplinary proceedings
  • Termination, suspension, and retrenchment

  • Provident Fund and gratuity matters
  • Regularisation and pay revision disputes

Revenue & Land Laws

Handling of land and revenue disputes under state revenue laws and before revenue courts.

Key Focus & Remedies

  • Mutation and title correction matters

  • Government land allotment disputes

Arbitration & Mediation

Alternative dispute resolution — helping parties resolve disputes efficiently outside the court system.

  • Court-annexed mediation and conciliation
