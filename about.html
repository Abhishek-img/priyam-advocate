Professional Profile

An Advocate Firm Committed to Justice

PALM LEGAL is an established law firm practising before the Supreme Court of India, various High Courts, and District Courts across the country. Led by partners Priyam Agarwal and Lovesh Mamnani, our practice spans a wide spectrum of legal domains, combining deep doctrinal knowledge with practical courtroom experience.

Over the years of practice, our chambers have built a reputation for meticulous case preparation, persuasive oral arguments, and unwavering dedication to our clients' causes. We approach every matter — however routine or complex — with the same degree of care, precision, and thoroughness.

Academic & Doctrinal Foundation

The legal foundations of PALM LEGAL are anchored in comprehensive legal education from premier law institutions in India. Rigorous training in both procedural jurisprudence and substantive civil, criminal, and constitutional doctrines forms the cornerstone of every advisory and litigation brief undertaken by our firm.

Professional Practice & Apex Court Presence

Our practice encompasses civil litigation, criminal defence, constitutional matters, family law, corporate disputes, and service law. The firm regularly appears before the Supreme Court of India, drafting, filing, and arguing Writ Petitions, Special Leave Petitions (SLPs), and Civil Appeals on behalf of individuals, business entities, and organizations across India.

In addition to litigation, PALM LEGAL provides comprehensive legal opinions, drafts commercial agreements, petitions, and pleadings, and advises clients on dispute prevention and risk management — offering a holistic legal service beyond the courtroom.

Philosophy & Approach

At PALM LEGAL, we firmly believe that access to justice is a fundamental right. We work to make quality legal representation accessible, transparent, and responsive to all our clients. Our approach is deeply collaborative — we take the time to understand the complete factual background of a matter, explain the legal position clearly, and work with clients to develop the most effective strategy.

Integrity, transparency, and client confidence are the pillars of our practice. We are committed to maintaining the highest ethical standards as mandated by the Bar Council of India and to upholding the dignity and solemn responsibility of the legal profession.
